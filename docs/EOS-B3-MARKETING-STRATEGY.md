# B3 — Marketing Strategy

**EyeOnSite** · Manti Labs · May 2026 · v0.1-DRAFT

---

## Growth Philosophy

EyeOnSite's marketing follows the same version logic as the product. Don't spend money before validating demand. Don't scale channels before proving they convert. Every dollar has a measurable job.

---

## V0 Marketing: Zero Budget, Maximum Signal (Month 1)

**Budget:** $0 (sweat equity only)

### Channels

| Channel | Action | Metric |
|---------|--------|--------|
| **Diaspora WhatsApp groups** | Join 10+. Post personal story. Offer first check free. | DMs received, tasks requested |
| **Diaspora Facebook groups** | Join 5+. Same approach. | Post engagement, DMs |
| **Personal LinkedIn** | Weekly posts: founder story, problem statement, first inspection. | Impressions, comments, DMs |
| **Personal X** | Thread: "I walked to a construction site in Accra..." | Retweets, followers, DMs |
| **MEST network** | Personal outreach to alumni with diaspora connections | Intros, referrals |
| **Church communities** | Ghanaian churches in UK (Accra corridor) — word of mouth | Referrals |

### V0 Success = Channel Discovery

The goal is NOT scale. It's discovering which channel produces the best clients:
- Which diaspora community responded most?
- UK-Ghana or US-Ghana or Canada-Ghana?
- Facebook or WhatsApp or LinkedIn?
- Construction or property or land?

## V1 Marketing: Prove the Loop ($100–$300/month)

**Budget:** $100–$300/month (from Farmerline salary)

### Channels (Ranked by Expected ROI)

**Tier 1: Free, high-trust channels**

| Channel | Strategy | CAC Target |
|---------|----------|-----------|
| **Referral program** | Every client gets a referral link. Refer a friend → first task free for both. | $0 (self-funding via LTV) |
| **WhatsApp community** | EyeOnSite diaspora WhatsApp group. Share updates, evidence examples, client stories. | $0 |
| **Content marketing** | LinkedIn (2x/week), X (3x/week), Medium/blog (2x/month). SEO-optimized posts about construction monitoring, diaspora investment. | $0 (time only) |
| **Partnership referrals** | Diaspora real estate agents, construction consultants, remittance agents who recommend EyeOnSite to clients. 10% referral commission. | 10% of first task value |

**Tier 2: Paid, targeted channels**

| Channel | Strategy | Budget | CAC Target |
|---------|----------|--------|-----------|
| **Facebook/Instagram ads** | Targeting: Ghanaians aged 30–55 in UK, US, Canada. Interest: Ghana real estate, building in Ghana, Ghana property. Creative: before/after evidence report, "see your house" headline. | $100–$200/mo | $5–$15 |
| **Google Ads** | Keywords: "verify construction Ghana", "check building progress Accra", "property verification Ghana diaspora". Small test budget. | $50–$100/mo | $10–$20 |

### Funnel: Diaspora Client

```
AWARENESS → CONSIDERATION → CONVERSION → RETENTION → REFERRAL

Awareness:
  Social media content, Facebook ads, WhatsApp groups, word of mouth
  Metric: impressions, group memberships, followers

Consideration:
  Landing page visit → "How it works" → evidence report examples
  Metric: landing page visits, time on page, scroll depth

Conversion:
  Create account → Create first task → Fund escrow
  Metric: signup rate, task creation rate, escrow funding rate
  Friction points: trust (will I get my money back?), price, app download

Retention:
  Evidence delivered → Client satisfied → Monthly recurring task
  Metric: repeat task rate, monthly active clients, NPS

Referral:
  Happy client → shares with friends → referral link
  Metric: referral rate, viral coefficient
```

### Key Activation Metrics (V1)

| Metric | Target | Measurement |
|--------|--------|-------------|
| Landing page → signup | 5–10% | Firebase Analytics |
| Signup → first task created | 30–40% | Firestore events |
| Task created → escrow funded | 70–80% | Payment completion rate |
| First task → second task | 40–50% | Retention cohort |
| Client → referral | 15–20% | Referral link clicks/conversions |
| CAC (blended) | < $10 | Total spend / new paying clients |
| Payback period | < 2 tasks | Revenue per client / CAC |

## V3 Marketing: Enterprise + Fundraise ($2,000–$5,000/month)

### Enterprise Marketing

| Channel | Strategy |
|---------|----------|
| **Direct sales** | Identify top 20 fintech lenders in Ghana, Nigeria, Kenya. Personal outreach. Pilot offer: 50 free verifications. |

| **Developer content** | Technical blog posts: "How to integrate EyeOnSite verification into your loan decisioning pipeline." |
| **Insurance conferences** | Insurance Society of Ghana, Kenya Insurance Industry Association events. Sponsor a panel. |

### Fundraise Marketing

| Asset | Purpose |
|-------|---------|
| **Pitch deck** (12 slides) | Problem → solution → market size → product demo → traction → business model → team → ask |
| **Data room** | All EOS docs, financial projections, client testimonials, inspector data, evidence quality metrics |

## SEO Strategy (V1+)

### Target Keywords

| Keyword | Volume (est) | Difficulty | Priority |
|---------|-------------|-----------|---------|
| "verify construction Ghana" | Low | Low | V1 |
| "property verification Ghana diaspora" | Very low | Very low | V1 |
| "building inspection Accra" | Low | Low | V1 |
| "check construction progress remotely" | Low | Medium | V1 |
| "diaspora property investment Africa" | Medium | Medium | V2 |
| "land verification Lagos" | Low | Medium | V2 |
| "property fraud Nigeria diaspora" | Medium | Low | V2 |
| "remote construction monitoring" | Medium | High | V3 |
| "field verification API Africa" | Very low | Very low | V3 |

### SEO Approach

- V1: Blog content targeting long-tail keywords. Minimal competition.
- V2: Build topical authority around "diaspora property verification." 20+ articles.
- V3: Technical content targeting enterprise keywords. API documentation as SEO asset.

**Note:** SEO is a secondary channel. Primary discovery is social/community/word-of-mouth. Compose for Web landing page has SEO limitations (CSR) — mitigate with prerender.io or consider a separate static HTML blog.
