# B2 — Messaging Playbook

**EyeOnSite** · Manti Labs · May 2026 · v0.1-DRAFT

---

## Messaging Architecture

Three layers of messaging, each serving a different purpose:

### Layer 1: Category Message (Why this matters)

"Africa's diaspora sends $100B home every year. The money moves. The trust doesn't."

*Used in: hero sections, pitch decks, press, investor conversations. Establishes the problem at scale.*

### Layer 2: Brand Message (What we do about it)

"EyeOnSite puts independent eyes on the ground — so you know your money did what it's supposed to do."

*Used in: about pages, marketing emails, social bios. Establishes our role.*

### Layer 3: Product Messages (How it works, specifically)

Per-audience, per-vertical, per-channel. See tables below.

## Audience-Specific Messaging

### Audience 1: Diaspora Individual — Building a House

**Their state of mind:** Anxious. They've sent significant money (often £20K–£50K+) and can't see what's happening. They may suspect something but don't want to confront family. They feel helpless.

**Primary message:** "See your house before you fly home."

**Supporting messages:**
- "Your contractor sends WhatsApp photos. We send GPS-verified proof."
- "Monthly progress reports with 12+ timestamped photos, checklist, and location verification."
- "Your money is held in escrow until you're satisfied with the evidence."
- "Real inspectors, real photos, real coordinates. Not a cousin with a phone."

**Objection handling:**
| Objection | Response |
|-----------|----------|
| "I trust my family / contractor" | "Trust and verify aren't opposites. Verification protects everyone — including your contractor's reputation." |
| "It's too expensive" | "You're spending £30,000 on a house. The monthly check costs £20. That's insurance for your life savings." |
| "I can just fly home and check" | "A round-trip flight is £600+. EyeOnSite is £20 and takes 4 hours, not 4 days." |
| "What if the inspector is also corrupt?" | "Our inspectors are ID-verified, background-checked, and never know who the client is. GPS and timestamps can't be faked. And your money's in escrow — you don't pay until you're satisfied." |

### Audience 2: Diaspora Individual — Buying Property

**Their state of mind:** Excited but terrified. The Instagram ads show beautiful plots and apartments. The developer promises everything. But they've heard the fraud stories. 70% of Lagos property purchases are diaspora — and the fraud rate is staggering.

**Primary message:** "Know it's real before you wire the money."

**Supporting messages:**
- "Our inspector physically visits the site. Checks the land exists. Photographs boundary markers. Verifies the address matches."
- "£40 to verify a £200,000 property is the cheapest insurance you'll ever buy."
- "In 2024, Lagos recovered ₦478 million from property fraud. Don't be next."

### Audience 3: Diaspora Individual — Managing Farm or Rental

**Primary message:** "Your farm. Your eyes. Even from London."

**Supporting messages:**
- "Quarterly crop checks with GPS-verified photos, growth stage assessment, and input tracking."
- "Know if your rental property is maintained, occupied, and earning what your agent claims."

### Audience 4: Enterprise — Fintech Lender

**Their state of mind:** Pragmatic. NPL rates are up. They need field verification to reduce fraud and improve loan quality. They're evaluating cost per verification vs. cost of bad loans.

**Primary message:** "Field truth for every loan application."

**Supporting messages:**
- "Verify that the shop, inventory, and collateral declared on loan applications actually exist — before you disburse."
- "API-triggered verification. Evidence package returned in hours, not days. Integrates with your loan decision engine."
- "$3–$10 per verification. Cheaper than a loan officer. Faster than a site visit. More reliable than self-reported data."
- "Reduce NPL rates by catching fabricated collateral before disbursement."

### Audience 5: Enterprise — Insurer

**Primary message:** "Independent claims verification at the speed your policyholders expect."

**Supporting messages:**
- "On-demand field verification: motor accident scenes, property damage, facility checks."
- "Evidence package with GPS-stamped photos, structured assessment, and fraud scoring."
- "WeGoLook proved this model in the US. We're bringing it to Africa."

### Audience 6: Inspector Candidates

**Their state of mind:** Looking for flexible income. May be a student, surveyor, gig worker, or field agent. Want something that pays well, is interesting, and fits around their schedule.

**Primary message:** "Get paid to be someone's eyes on the ground."

**Supporting messages:**
- "Earn GHS 50–200 per task. Accept when you want. Work when you want."
- "All you need is a smartphone and the ability to follow a checklist."
- "Build your reputation. Top-rated inspectors get the best tasks and the highest pay."
- "You're not just earning money — you're protecting someone's family investment."
- "Instant payout to MTN MoMo after every accepted task."

## Channel Messaging

### Social Media (X / Twitter)

**Content pillars (3x/week minimum):**

| Pillar | Percentage | Example |
|--------|-----------|---------|
| **Problem stories** | 30% | "A UK-based Ghanaian sent £40K to build a house. 14 months later, it's half-finished. The contractor stopped returning calls. This happens every day." |
| **Product / how it works** | 25% | "How EyeOnSite works: 1. Post a task 2. We match a verified inspector 3. They visit your site within hours 4. You get GPS-stamped photos + checklist 5. Money's in escrow until you approve" |
| **Social proof / evidence** | 20% | "[Screenshot of evidence report] This is what a real construction progress check looks like. 12 photos, GPS-verified, submitted at 14:32 UTC." |
| **Founder story** | 15% | "I'm building EyeOnSite because I watched friends lose money on construction projects they couldn't monitor. I live in Accra. I started by being the inspector myself." |
| **Industry / data** | 10% | "$100B in African remittances. $4.7B to Ghana alone. How much of that builds what it's supposed to?" |

### LinkedIn

**Content type:** Longer-form. Professional audience. Mix of personal founder narrative + business traction + industry insight.

**Posting cadence:** 3x/week

**Sample posts:**

*Founder story:*
"Three months ago, I walked to a construction site in Tema with a smartphone and a checklist. The house belonged to a nurse in London who hadn't heard from her contractor in 6 weeks.

What I found: workers on site, materials stacked, the foundation was solid. She cried on the phone when I sent the photos. Not because something was wrong — because she finally knew.

That was EyeOnSite task #1. We've now done [X] more. Here's what I've learned about building trust infrastructure for Africa's diaspora."

*Product launch:*
"Today we're opening EyeOnSite to Accra.

The problem: diaspora families send money for construction, property, and farms — and have no way to verify what happens.

The solution: on-demand verified inspectors. GPS-stamped evidence. Escrow-protected payments.

Think Bolt, but for physical verification.

Starting in Accra with construction monitoring. GHS 30–60 per task. First check is free.

Link in comments."

### WhatsApp / Facebook Groups (Diaspora Communities)

**Tone:** Personal, conversational. NOT salesy. You're a community member sharing something useful, not a brand pushing a product.

**Do:** Share personal story. Ask questions. Offer value first ("here's what to look for when hiring a contractor in Accra"). Mention EyeOnSite naturally as a solution.

**Don't:** Lead with the product. Use marketing language. Post the same message in 10 groups. Spam.

**Sample message:**

"Hi everyone, my name is Maku. I'm a developer based in Accra. I've been hearing so many stories about construction projects that go wrong — contractors disappearing, money wasted, no one to check what's happening on site.

I started doing physical site inspections for diaspora families as a service. I go to the construction site, take GPS-verified photos, fill a checklist, and send a report. It's been really eye-opening.

I've now built an app called EyeOnSite that connects diaspora families with verified local inspectors who can do these checks. Your money is held in escrow until you're satisfied.

If anyone is currently building back home and wants a check, I'm offering the first inspection free. Just DM me."

### Email (Newsletter / Transactional)

**Newsletter (monthly):**
- Subject line formula: [specific detail] + [benefit/question]
- Examples:
  - "12 photos, 1 truth: how we verified a Tema construction site"
  - "Your contractor says the roof is done. Our inspector says otherwise."
  - "3 things to check before buying land in Lagos"
- Body: 80% value (story, insight, data), 20% product mention
- CTA: "Start your first check" or "See how it works"

**Transactional emails:**
- Task created: "Your verification task is live. We're matching an inspector now."
- Inspector matched: "Good news — [Inspector Name] is heading to your site. ETA: [time]."
- Evidence ready: "Your evidence is ready for review. [X] photos, all GPS-verified."
- Evidence accepted: "Task complete. Your inspector was paid. Here's your report."
- Weekly summary: "This week: [X] tasks completed, [Y] photos verified, average quality score [Z]."

## Competitor Messaging Differentiation

### vs. "Just ask your cousin / the contractor"

"Your cousin means well. But can they timestamp every photo with GPS coordinates? Can they hold funds in escrow? Can they compare this month's progress against last month's standard? EyeOnSite isn't about replacing trust — it's about adding proof."

### vs. LandSafe / Mantel Verify (managed services)

"Managed services are great for high-value, one-time verifications. But if you're monitoring construction monthly for 12 months, you need a platform — not a concierge. EyeOnSite: on-demand, repeatable, affordable."

### vs. Hiring a local surveyor directly

"A surveyor costs $100–$300 per visit. EyeOnSite costs $20–$50. And your money's in escrow — if the inspection isn't good enough, you don't pay. Try negotiating that with a freelancer."
