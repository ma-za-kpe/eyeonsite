# A2 — City Launch Playbook

**EyeOnSite** · Manti Labs · May 2026 · v0.1-DRAFT

## Purpose

Repeatable 8-week playbook for launching EyeOnSite in a new city. Designed so Maku (or a city launcher hire) can execute it without reinventing the process each time.

## The 8-Week Launch Playbook

### Week 1–2: Ground Truth

**Goal:** Understand the local market. You can't launch from a spreadsheet.

- [ ] Fly in / be physically present for at minimum 5 days
- [ ] Walk 10+ active construction sites. Photograph. Talk to foremen. Understand the building culture.
- [ ] Visit 3+ real estate agencies. Understand diaspora buying patterns. Who are the fraud victims?
- [ ] Meet 5+ potential inspectors (engineering students, surveyors, gig workers). What do they earn? What would motivate them?
- [ ] Join local WhatsApp groups (construction, real estate, gig work, university)
- [ ] Identify the diaspora Facebook/WhatsApp groups for this city's corridor (e.g., UK-Ghanaians in Kumasi, US-Nigerians building in Lagos)
- [ ] Map mobile money infrastructure: which providers, coverage, payout speed
- [ ] Identify 3–5 "anchor sites" — active construction projects where you could demonstrate the product

**Deliverable:** City intelligence brief (2 pages). Go/no-go for Week 3.

### Week 3–4: Inspector Pipeline

**Goal:** 15–20 verified inspectors ready to receive tasks on launch day.

- [ ] Post inspector recruitment ads: university notice boards, WhatsApp gig groups, Facebook, X
- [ ] Referral bonus: existing inspectors (from other cities) refer friends → GHS 20 per verified referral
- [ ] Run 3 recruitment events: university campus talk (15 min, "earn GHS 100+/week doing site inspections"), gig worker meetup
- [ ] Screen applicants: phone interview, ID verification, background check initiated
- [ ] Onboard top 15–20: training module completion, quiz, test task
- [ ] Test tasks: each inspector completes 1 real test task at a nearby construction site
- [ ] Quality review: manually grade all test task evidence packages
- [ ] Activate passing inspectors (target: 80% pass rate from test task)
- [ ] Create city-specific WhatsApp group for inspector communication

**Deliverable:** 15+ verified, trained, tested inspectors. Inspector WhatsApp group active.

### Week 5–6: Demand Generation

**Goal:** 20+ clients aware and 5+ with tasks ready for launch week.

- [ ] Diaspora corridor marketing: post in identified Facebook/WhatsApp groups (3x/week, personal story format)
- [ ] LinkedIn content: "We're launching in [City] — here's what we learned about diaspora construction trust"
- [ ] X thread: city-specific construction/property fraud stories + "there's now a solution"
- [ ] Personal outreach: email/WhatsApp 30+ diaspora contacts with ties to this city
- [ ] Early access list: landing page with city-specific messaging ("EyeOnSite is now in [City]")
- [ ] Partnership outreach: contact 3–5 remittance companies, diaspora organizations, church groups active in this corridor
- [ ] Press/media: pitch story to 1–2 diaspora media outlets or blogs
- [ ] Referral incentive: first task free (platform covers inspector payout, max $30)

**Deliverable:** 20+ signups on early access list. 5+ confirmed first-week tasks.

### Week 7: Soft Launch

**Goal:** Complete 10+ tasks. Everything works end-to-end. No fires.

- [ ] Launch with curated first batch: hand-pick best inspectors for first tasks
- [ ] Monitor every task in real-time: watch matching, capture, submission, review
- [ ] Maku personally reviews every evidence package for first 10 tasks
- [ ] Same-day client check-in: "How was the experience? Anything missing?"
- [ ] Same-day inspector check-in: "Any issues? Was the task clear? How was payout?"
- [ ] Fix bugs in real-time
- [ ] Document every issue in GitHub Issues

**Deliverable:** 10+ completed tasks. Bug list. Client feedback. Inspector feedback.

### Week 8: Public Launch

**Goal:** Open to all. Marketing push. Target: 30+ tasks in first month.

- [ ] Remove early access gate (open sign-up)
- [ ] Marketing push: all diaspora channels, LinkedIn, X, partnerships
- [ ] PR: "EyeOnSite launches in [City]" — LinkedIn post, X thread, blog post
- [ ] Track metrics daily: tasks created, tasks completed, dispute rate, inspector utilization, revenue
- [ ] Weekly ops review: what's working, what's broken, what needs to change
- [ ] Inspector quality review: flag underperformers, celebrate top performers

**Deliverable:** 30+ tasks in Month 1. Metrics dashboard live. Ops rhythm established.

## City-Specific Adaptations

### Accra (V1 Launch City)

- **Corridors:** UK→Ghana (primary), US→Ghana, Canada→Ghana, Germany→Ghana
- **Inspector pool:** KNUST engineering students, University of Ghana geography students, Bolt drivers, field agent networks
- **Payment:** MTN MoMo (dominant), Vodafone Cash, AirtelTigo Money, bank transfer
- **Language:** English (primary), Twi (inspector communication)
- **Safety:** Generally safe. Avoid: certain areas at night. No major no-go zones for daytime inspection.
- **Regulatory:** Register with Data Protection Commission (Act 843). No gig economy regulation currently.
- **Competition:** None doing marketplace verification. LandSafe and others are Nigeria-only.

### Lagos (V2 Candidate)

- **Corridors:** US→Nigeria (massive), UK→Nigeria, Canada→Nigeria
- **Inspector pool:** University of Lagos, Yaba College of Technology, massive gig economy, field agents
- **Payment:** Bank transfer (more common than MoMo in Lagos), OPay, PalmPay growing
- **Language:** English, Pidgin English (critical for inspector communication)
- **Safety:** Higher risk. Traffic is a major ops challenge. Some areas unsafe. Inspectors need clear no-go zone guidance.
- **Regulatory:** NDPA registration. Monitor CBN guidance on escrow. Nigeria has active consumer protection.
- **Competition:** LandSafe, Mantel Verify, LANDIZ all present. Differentiate on: on-demand speed, multi-vertical, lower price point.
- **Special challenges:** Traffic means longer task completion times. Build in buffer. "Lagos time" is real — inspector SLAs need adjustment.

### Nairobi (V2 Candidate)

- **Corridors:** US→Kenya, UK→Kenya, Canada→Kenya, Australia→Kenya
- **Inspector pool:** University of Nairobi, Jomo Kenyatta University, strong surveyor community, Uber/Bolt drivers
- **Payment:** M-Pesa (dominant, instant, ubiquitous). Best mobile money infrastructure of any target market.
- **Language:** English, Swahili
- **Safety:** Generally safe for daytime operations. Some estates require caution. Inspector safety briefing important.
- **Regulatory:** Data Protection Act 2019 — register with ODPC. Kenya has no mandatory developer escrow (our value prop is strong here).
- **Competition:** The Diaspora Access (traditional, not tech-enabled). Light competition.
- **Special advantages:** M-Pesa makes inspector payouts instant and frictionless. Best mobile money UX.

## Launch Metrics Dashboard

Track daily for first 60 days post-launch:

| Metric | Target (Month 1) | Target (Month 2) | Red Flag |
|--------|-----------------|-----------------|----------|
| Tasks created | 30+ | 60+ | < 10 |
| Tasks completed | 25+ | 50+ | < 8 |
| Completion rate | > 80% | > 85% | < 60% |
| Dispute rate | < 20% | < 15% | > 30% |
| Active inspectors | 10+ | 15+ | < 5 |
| Inspector utilization (tasks/inspector/week) | 1.5+ | 2.0+ | < 0.5 |
| Repeat clients | 3+ | 8+ | 0 |
| Average evidence quality score | > 0.65 | > 0.70 | < 0.50 |
| Average client rating | > 3.8 | > 4.0 | < 3.0 |
| Revenue | $100+ | $250+ | $0 |

**City kill criteria:** If after 8 weeks post-launch, the city has < 50 total completed tasks AND < 5 active inspectors AND 0 repeat clients → pause operations, reallocate resources.
