## The EyeOnSite Brand in One Paragraph

EyeOnSite's brand is built on one idea: **trust made tangible**. The visual identity uses Forest green (grounded, African, trustworthy) with Savanna Gold accents (warmth, value). The logo fuses a map pin with an eye — verification at a verified location. The voice is direct, warm, grounded, and brave: we speak like a trusted friend who happens to be an engineer. We never use corporate jargon, never sugarcoat the problem, and always lead with specific numbers and real places. The tagline — "Trust, verified." — says everything in two words.
