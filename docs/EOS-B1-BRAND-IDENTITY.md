## Brand Essence

**One sentence:** EyeOnSite gives diaspora investors independent eyes on the ground — so the money they send home does what it's supposed to do.

Unlike informal WhatsApp checks or expensive managed services, EyeOnSite combines the speed of Bolt with the accountability of an escrow, using verified local inspectors you can trust.

## Logo

### The Mark: Pin × Eye

The EyeOnSite logo fuses two symbols:

1. **The map pin** — physical location, specificity, "right here, right now"
2. **The eye** — sight, verification, watching, presence

Together they say: *someone is watching, at this exact place*. The pin contains the eye, because the verification only matters at the verified location.

### Logo Construction

- The pin follows a teardrop/shield silhouette with the point downward
- The eye sits at the pin's center of gravity (slightly above vertical center)
- The eye is almond-shaped with a circular iris containing a pupil and a light reflection dot
- The pin's green fill represents the iris color when the eye and pin merge

### Logo Variants

| Variant | Use Case | Specification |
|---------|----------|--------------|
| **Primary** (pin + wordmark) | Website header, documents, pitch decks, press | Full color pin mark + "EyeOnSite" wordmark to the right. "by Manti Labs" subtitle optional. |
| **Icon only** (pin) | App icon, favicon, avatar, small spaces | Pin mark without wordmark. Minimum size: 24×24px. |
| **App icon** (pin on dark background) | Android/iOS app icon, Play Store, App Store | Pin mark centered on Deep Forest (#022B1E) rounded square. |
| **Badge** (white pin on green circle) | Stickers, merch, pins, stamps, watermarks | White pin mark on Forest (#0B6E4F) circle. |
| **Monochrome** | Single-color contexts, fax, stamps, engraving | Pin mark in single foreground color. |
| **Outline** | Light backgrounds, subtle branding, loading states | Pin outline only, stroke-based. |

### Logo Don'ts

- Do NOT rotate the pin (the point always faces down — it's a location marker)
- Do NOT separate the eye from the pin
- Do NOT change the eye's proportions relative to the pin
- Do NOT add a drop shadow, glow, or gradient
- Do NOT use the wordmark without the pin mark in any primary placement
- Do NOT place the logo on a busy photographic background without a clear zone
- Minimum clear space: 1x the pin height on all sides

### Color Rationale

**Why green?** Green is universal trust (banking, health, growth). It's the color of the land EyeOnSite verifies. It's the color of the GPS "verified" indicator. And it's deeply African — lush landscapes, agriculture, the "green in the flag" of Ghana, Nigeria, Kenya, Uganda. Green says: grounded, trustworthy, alive.

**Why gold accent?** Gold represents value — the money diaspora families send home. It's warm against the cool green. It's aspirational without being flashy. And it references the "Gold Coast" (Ghana's colonial name, reclaimed with pride).

**Why NOT blue?** Every fintech uses blue (NALA, LemFi, Flutterwave, Paystack). EyeOnSite is not a fintech — it's a trust company. Green differentiates instantly.

## Typography

### Type Stack

| Role | Font | Weight | Size Range | Use |
|------|------|--------|-----------|-----|
| **Headlines** | DM Sans | Medium (500) | 28–48px | Page titles, hero text, section headers |
| **Subheadlines** | DM Sans | Medium (500) | 18–24px | Card titles, section subheads |
| **Body** | DM Sans | Regular (400) | 14–16px | Paragraphs, descriptions, UI text |
| **Labels** | DM Sans | Medium (500) | 12–14px | Buttons, tags, badges, form labels |
| **Code/Data** | JetBrains Mono | Regular (400) | 12–14px | Task IDs, GPS coordinates, code, technical data |

### Font Selection Rationale

**DM Sans** — Geometric sans-serif. Clean, modern, highly readable on screens. Similar warmth to Google Sans but open source. Works in Latin scripts that cover English, French (West Africa), and Swahili romanization. Not too cold (like Inter) or too quirky (like Poppins).

**JetBrains Mono** — For the technical data that underpins trust: GPS coordinates, timestamps, task IDs, evidence quality scores. The monospace font says "this is precise data, not marketing." And it's the font Maku codes in — authentic to the builder.

## Brand Voice

### Four Pillars

**1. Direct** — Say what you mean. No jargon, no corporate filler, no passive voice. "Your inspector is 12 minutes away" not "Your verification request has been assigned to a qualified professional."

**2. Warm** — We're talking to people protecting their families' investments. Empathy first. "We know how hard you worked for this money" not "Our platform ensures verification compliance."

**3. Grounded** — Specific numbers, real places, concrete details. "GPS-verified at 5.6037°N, 0.1870°W, 14:32 UTC" not "Our advanced technology ensures accuracy." We prove, we don't claim.

**4. Brave** — We name the problem directly. "Contractor fraud costs diaspora families millions every year" not "There are occasional challenges in remote project management." We don't sugarcoat because our users don't have that luxury.

### Voice in Practice

| Context | Do This | Not This |
|---------|---------|----------|
| **Error message** | "Payment didn't go through. Try again or use a different card." | "An error has occurred in the payment processing subsystem. Please contact support." |
| **Evidence accepted** | "Your inspector was at 5.6°N, 0.2°W at 2:32 PM. 12 photos, all verified. Your house is at lintel stage." | "Your verification request has been completed and approved. Please review the associated documentation." |
| **Marketing headline** | "See your house before you fly home." | "Comprehensive remote property verification solutions." |
| **Onboarding** | "Welcome. Let's make sure your money does what it's supposed to." | "Thank you for choosing EyeOnSite, the premier verification platform." |
| **Dispute notification** | "Something's off with this inspection. We're looking into it. You'll hear from us within 72 hours." | "A dispute has been initiated. The dispute resolution team will investigate per our standard operating procedures." |

| Channel | Tone Adjustment |
|---------|----------------|
| **App notifications** | Ultra-concise. One sentence. Action-oriented. "Inspector matched. 12 min away." |

## Photography and Imagery

### Photo Style

**What to shoot:**
- Active construction sites in Accra/Lagos/Nairobi (the real thing, not stock)
- Inspectors on the ground — walking to sites, using the app, holding phones up to buildings
- Evidence screenshots — GPS-overlaid photos, checklist UIs, map views
- Diaspora moments — someone checking their phone in London/Houston/Toronto, seeing a report
- Before/after comparisons — "what the contractor sent on WhatsApp" vs. "what EyeOnSite verified"

**Photo style rules:**
- Natural light, real locations, real people
- Slightly desaturated — we're not a lifestyle brand, we're a trust brand
- Green tint/grade overlay in post-production (subtle, 10-15%)
- No stock photos ever (the irony of a verification company using fake photos would be lethal)
- Show the app on real devices in real hands
- Construction sites should look like construction sites — messy, real, in-progress

**What NOT to shoot:**
- Glossy corporate handshakes
- Generic "African city skyline" stock
- Models pretending to be inspectors
- Perfectly staged construction sites
- Before photos that are too bad (don't mock real people's situations)

### Illustration Style (for marketing, not the app)

- Simple, geometric, flat illustrations
- Green + Gold + Ink palette only
- Used for: explanatory diagrams, social media graphics, pitch deck visuals
- Style reference: Duolingo-level simplicity but with EyeOnSite's architectural/map/pin motifs
- Icon system: outline style, 2px stroke, matching the logo's outline variant
