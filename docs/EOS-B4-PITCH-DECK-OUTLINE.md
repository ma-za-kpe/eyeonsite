### Slide 2: The Problem

**$100 billion sent home. Zero way to verify.**

- Africa receives ~$100B/year in remittances (World Bank)
- Ghana alone: $4.7B–$6.6B (7% of GDP)
- A significant portion funds physical projects: houses, land, farms, businesses
- The money moves in minutes. Verification takes months — or never happens.

**Visual:** The Ama story. London nurse. £40K. Contractor disappears. House half-finished.

"56% of diaspora investors cite corruption as their #1 barrier to investing back home."

---

### Slide 3: Why Now

1. **Remittance rails are solved** — NALA ($40M Series A), LemFi ($1B+/month), Flutterwave. Moving money is cheap and fast. Nobody solved what happens after.
2. **Mobile money is ubiquitous** — MTN MoMo, M-Pesa make inspector payouts frictionless.
3. **Post-COVID** — remote oversight is the new normal.
4. **Africa's lending explosion** — 40M Nigerian MSMEs, digital lending booming. Every loan needs field verification.
5. **KMP is production-ready** — one developer can ship Android + iOS + Web.

---

### Slide 4: The Solution

**Bolt for physical verification.**

1. Client posts a task (location, instructions, budget)
2. Funds go into escrow
3. Nearby verified inspector gets pinged
4. Inspector travels to site, captures GPS-stamped evidence in-app
5. Client reviews evidence package
6. Accepts → escrow released. Disputes → platform mediates.

**Visual:** Task lifecycle diagram + real evidence report screenshot
